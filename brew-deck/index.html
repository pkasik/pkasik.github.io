Grind guide

Comandante C40 MK4 · Red Clix

≈ 15 µm per click

Comandante C40 MK4 with Red Clix. Two clicks (30 µm) is the smallest change worth tasting; three clicks is a real step, about one Casa number.

Casa 15 ≈ 50 clicks

The deck converts each Casa setting through its micron estimate. Expect to land 2–4 clicks finer than the number shown: the C40 makes fewer fines, so it needs a little less gap for the same extraction.

Fast, thin or sour

2 clicks finer, then 3 more if the cup doesn't move. Change temperature only once the drawdown lands in the window.

Slow, drying or bitter

2 clicks coarser, or drop 1°C. On naturals that read winey after a fast brew, go finer first, not cooler.

Lagom Casa

≈ 50 µm per number

10 numbers per rotation, so one full turn is about 500 µm. Half a number (25 µm) is the smallest change worth tasting; a full number is a real step.

15 ≈ 750 µm

Your anchor sits at the coarse end of the usual V60 window (600–800 µm). For most of these coffees the working range is 13.5–15.

Fast, thin or sour

Grind ½ number finer, then a full number if the cup doesn't move. Change temperature only once the drawdown lands in the window.

Slow, drying or bitter

Grind ½ number coarser, or drop 1°C. On naturals that read winey after a fast brew, go finer first, not cooler.

Micron figures are estimates from published grind charts (Casa ≈ 50 µm per number; Red Clix ≈ 15 µm per click), not manufacturer specifications; use them to compare settings, not as absolutes.
