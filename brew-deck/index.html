Grind guide

Comandante C40 MK4 · Red Clix

≈ 15 µm per click

Comandante C40 MK4 with Red Clix. Two clicks (30 µm) is the smallest change worth tasting; three clicks is a real step, about one Casa number.

Casa 15 ≈ 50 clicks

The deck converts each Casa setting through its micron estimate. Expect to land 2–4 clicks finer than the number shown: the C40 makes fewer fines, so it needs a little less gap for the same extraction.

Fast, thin or sour

2 clicks finer, then 3 more if the cup doesn't move. Change temperature only once the drawdown lands in the window.

Slow, drying or bitter

2 clicks coarser, or drop 1°C. On naturals that read winey after a fast brew, go finer first, not cooler.

Lagom Casa

≈ 10 µm per tick

Four tick marks between numbers, so a tick is a fifth of a number and a number is about 50 µm; 10 numbers per rotation. Settings read number.ticks: 14.2 is the 2nd tick after 14. One tick is the smallest change worth tasting; two is a real step.

15.0 ≈ 750 µm

Your anchor sits at the coarse end of the usual V60 window (600–800 µm). For most of these coffees the working range is 13.3 to 15.0; Honest Coffee Guide writes the same settings as rotation.number.tick, so 15.0 is their 1.5.0.

Fast, thin or sour

2 ticks finer, then a full number if the cup doesn't move. Change temperature only once the drawdown lands in the window.

Slow, drying or bitter

2 ticks coarser, or drop 1°C. On naturals that read winey after a fast brew, go finer first, not cooler.

Fellow Ode Gen 2 · stock Gen 2 burrs

≈ 30 µm per click

Dial 1–11 with two clicks between numbers, so "6.1" is one click past 6. A click is the smallest change worth tasting; a full number (≈ 90 µm) is a big step, nearly two Casa numbers.

Casa 15 ≈ 6.1

The deck converts each Casa setting through its micron estimate (Ode range ≈ 275–1160 µm). The Gen 2 flat burrs make a similar, clean particle spread to the Casa, so expect to land within a click of the number shown.

Fast, thin or sour

1 click finer, then 2 more if the cup doesn't move. Change temperature only once the drawdown lands in the window.

Slow, drying or bitter

1 click coarser, or drop 1°C. On naturals that read winey after a fast brew, go finer first, not cooler.

Micron figures are estimates from published grind charts (Casa ≈ 50 µm per number, 10 µm per tick; Red Clix ≈ 15 µm per click; Ode Gen 2 ≈ 30 µm per click), not manufacturer specifications; use them to compare settings, not as absolutes.
